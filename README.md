# Finite-dimensional Lehner edge estimates in Lean

This is a **partial formalization**, with proved intermediate results, of
Benoît Collins and Yuta Yamagishi, *A Sudakov–Fernique proof of Lehner-type edge
bounds for matrix-valued GUE sums*, [arXiv:2606.21137v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21137v2).
The full v2 PDF, including its proofs, was read before implementation.

**Theorem 7.2 is not proved.** Neither are the expected spectral-edge bounds,
the Wishart expectation estimate, the asymptotic corollaries, or the
identification with free spectral edges. There is no axiom asserting any of
these results and no theorem with additional assumptions standing in for them.

The development proves the two-fiber Stiefel increment inequality (Lemma 4.1),
the weighted trace arithmetic–geometric mean inequality, the density upper
bound against the exact variational `rhoPlus`, variational sign symmetry and
the left density inequality, rectangular Schatten estimates,
the deterministic block comparison estimate, and every finite restriction of
the Gaussian comparison with arbitrary deterministic drift. It also transports
Gaussian concentration to finite real Hilbert spaces and applies it to finite
restrictions of the spectral process.

## Build and verify

The toolchain is Lean **4.34.1** and Mathlib **v4.34.1**. All dependencies are
pinned in `lake-manifest.json`. Both suggested repositories currently support
this toolchain; their pinned commits are recorded in [PROVENANCE.md](docs/PROVENANCE.md).

```sh
lake update
lake build
lake env lean examples/Import.lean
lake env lean docs/Statements.lean
lake env lean scripts/Audit.lean
```

On a new checkout, Mathlib's update hook downloads its compiled cache. The
project's update hook applies a **name-only compatibility patch** to
Lean-Sudakov: `map_update_prod_pi` becomes
`LehnerSudakovCompat.map_update_prod_pi` in `GaussianVectorIBP.lean`.
The original name otherwise collides with a different global lemma exported
by lean-log-sobolev. No statement or proof is changed. Lake's warning that this
dependency has local changes is expected. The hook is idempotent.

All mathematical declarations are in the namespace `LehnerEdges`. Import
`LehnerEdges` for the complete proved development. See:

- [Formalization report](docs/FORMALIZATION.md): correspondence, precise scope,
  dependency diagram, and remaining obstacles.
- [Exact Lean statements](docs/statements.txt), generated by [Statements.lean](docs/Statements.lean).
- [Verification report](docs/VERIFICATION.md) and [recursive axiom audit](scripts/Audit.lean).
- [External provenance and licenses](docs/PROVENANCE.md).
